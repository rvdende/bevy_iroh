This page hosts a room. The link in the address bar is the invite, so open it in a second browser tab, on your phone, or send it to a friend and they will show up here.

Arrow keys to move, Enter to type a message, M to mute. Mic, camera and device settings are top right. Voices are positional, so walk up to someone to hear them better.
