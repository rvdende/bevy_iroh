You are in a room of your own. The address bar holds the invitation: anyone who opens this URL joins you, so try it in another browser, on your phone, or send it to someone. A desktop joins with the ticket in it, too.

Arrow keys move you. Enter opens the chat, Enter sends, Escape closes. M mutes. Top right: mute, your microphone level, the camera, and the device pickers. Each person is a sphere, with their picture and their words over it, and their voice comes from where they stand.
