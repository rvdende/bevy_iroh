bevy_iroh EXAMPLE Open in a second browser or share the link to try it with someone.

This page hosts a room. The link in the address bar is the invite, so open it in a second browser tab, on your phone, or send it to a friend and they will show up here.

Arrow keys to move, Enter to type a message, M to mute. Mic, camera, screen sharing and device settings are top right. "Share screen" asks the browser for a tab, window or screen, and whatever you pick hangs above your sphere for everyone until "Stop sharing". Voices are positional, so walk up to someone to hear them better.
