bevy_iroh

Share Bevy entities between peers over iroh, with voice and video. This page is the README, and the demos below are the crate's examples built for the browser.

webcamVoice and a camera: everyone's picture floats over their sphere. Asks for microphone and camera. voiceVoice only, with a mute button, a meter and device pickers. Asks for a microphone. cubeThe hello world: a cube each, driven with the arrow keys. Nothing to allow.

Open a demo and its address bar becomes the invitation: copy the URL and send it, and whoever opens it joins your room. Two tabs of your own work too. A desktop peer joins with the ticket in that URL: cargo run --example webcam --features ui,webrtc,v4l2 -- <ticket>. Browser peers talk to each other and to desktops over hole-punched WebRTC data channels; the first few seconds before that link is up go through a relay.
